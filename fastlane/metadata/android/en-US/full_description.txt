Pulse Music is a next-generation open-source music player and streaming application designed for pure, uninterrupted listening pleasure on Android and Windows. Built with Compose Multiplatform, Pulse delivers a silky-smooth, ad-free experience powered by advanced audio technologies.

Experience music like never before:

🛡️ LISTEN HEALTHY & HEARING PROTECTION (WHO 85dB)
• Built-in safe volume limiter automatically caps headphone output at 75% to prevent long-term acoustic damage and hearing fatigue.
• Live exposure intensity monitor displays active status badges (Safe, Moderate, Break Needed).
• Customizable ear fatigue break reminders (30m, 45m, 60m, 90m, 120m) prompt you to give your ears essential rest.

🧭 SONG DISCOVERY & SMART RADIO MIX
• Dynamic Discovery Mix slider in Settings and Queue sheet allows you to fine-tune the exact balance between familiar favorites and new streaming recommendations (0% familiar to 100% explore).
• Interactive Quick Picks Discovery preset badges (20%, 50%, 100%) on the Home screen header for curated musical variety.

⚡ ZERO-LOCK CONTINUOUS PLAYBACK ENGINE
• Asynchronous non-blocking reactive streams eliminate stuttering, playback buffering freezes, and track skip latency.
• Zero memory leaks or 1+ hour playback lockups for uninterrupted listening day and night.

🎤 REAL-TIME APPLE & SPOTIFY HYBRID SYNCED LYRICS
• Syllable-by-syllable and word-by-word progressive karaoke glow synced to the beat.
• Multilingual romanization for 12+ global scripts (Japanese Kanji/Kana, Korean Hangul, Chinese Pinyin, Tamil, Telugu, Hindi, Malayalam, Arabic).
• Floating mini capsule lyrics for seamless multitasking.

🎨 EXPRESSIVE MATERIAL 3 & AMOLED THEMES
• Dynamic ambient light extracted in real time from album art.
• Instant theme switching between Dark AMOLED (#000000), Pure Light, System Default, and Pastel Pearl Lavender.

🎚️ AUDIOPHILE EQUALIZER & SPATIAL AUDIO
• 10-Band professional audio equalizer with bass boost and virtualizer.
• AutoEq headphone presets calibrated for over 1,000+ headphone models.
• Spatial 8D audio and reverberation acoustic room modeling.

🚗 CONNECTIVITY & PLATFORMS
• Full Android Auto support for seamless in-car entertainment.
• Seamless cross-platform synchronization with the native Windows Desktop companion app.
• Sleep timer, offline song caching, SponsorBlock, and Return YouTube Dislike.
