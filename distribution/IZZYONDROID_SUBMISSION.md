Pulse Music is an open-source YouTube Music client featuring:
- Ad-free YouTube Music background playback
- Continuous playback engine with zero freezing during track skips and play/pause
- Real-time Apple Music & Spotify hybrid styled synchronized lyrics
- Offline caching and downloaded song storage
- Audio equalizer, spatial audio, and loudness enhancers
- Android Auto support
- Fastlane metadata and reproducible release builds
